MBA Global Program – IMAS
Category 1: Important FAQs / Degree & Certification
Q1: What degree will I receive upon completing the MBA Global program?
A1: Graduates receive a Post Graduate Diploma in Management (PGDM) from IMAS, which is AICTE-approved in India. Additionally, there's a pathway to complete an MBA from a UK university, such as Leeds Beckett University or Cardiff Metropolitan University, offering international recognition. (imas.ac.in)
Q2: Is the degree recognized internationally?
 A2: Yes, the program offers a UK pathway, ensuring international recognition of your MBA degree.
Q3: What is the duration of the program?
 A3: The MBA Global program spans two years, with the initial year completed at IMAS Kolkata and the subsequent year at a partner UK university.
Q4: Are there any prerequisites to enroll?
 A4: Applicants must have completed their graduation with 70% or above. Specific eligibility criteria can be found in the program brochure. (imas.ac.in)
Q5: Can I obtain a digital copy of my certificate?
 A5: Yes, digital certificates are typically provided upon completion of the program.

Category 2: Eligibility & Criteria
Q1: Who is eligible to apply for the MBA Global program?
 A1: Graduates from any discipline are eligible. For detailed eligibility criteria, please refer to the program brochure. (imas.ac.in)
Q2: Is prior work experience required?
 A2: The program does not explicitly mention a requirement for work experience. However, applicants with relevant experience may find the program beneficial.
Q3: What is the minimum educational qualification needed?
 A3: A graduate degree with 70% aggregate marks from a recognized institution is required.
Q4: Are there any age restrictions?
 A4: The program does not specify any age restrictions.
Q5: Can international students apply?
 A5: Yes, the program is open to international students, especially with the UK pathway option.

Category 3: Application Process
Q1: How can I apply for the MBA Global program?
 A1: Applications can be submitted online through the official IMAS website.
Q2: Is there an online application form?
 A2: Yes, the application form is available on the IMAS website.
Q3: What documents are required for the application?
 A3: Typically, the required documents include:
Graduation mark sheets and certificates


Passport-sized photographs


Identity proof


Address proof


Entrance exam scores 
Q4: Is there an application fee?
 A4: : Yes, there is an application fee of ₹500 to apply for the MBA Global program
Q5: How long does it take to process my application?
 A5: Application processing times can vary. For the most accurate information, please refer to the admissions section on the IMAS website or talk to our Admission Cell.
