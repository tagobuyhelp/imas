# 🎓 PGDM (Post Graduate Diploma in Management) FAQs

## 1. What is unique about the PGDM program at IMAS?
IMAS offers a holistic approach to management education, blending theoretical knowledge with practical insights. The curriculum is designed to equip students with the skills necessary to excel in the dynamic business world.

## 2. Is the PGDM program approved by any regulatory body?
Yes, the PGDM program at IMAS is approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), ensuring that the course meets national standards of quality education.

## 3. What specializations are available in the PGDM program?
IMAS offers both Regular and Executive Programs catering to the need and career-goal of individual aspirant:

### The Regular Programs offered by IMAS are:
**Post Graduate Diploma in Management (approved by AICTE)**, a two years full-time program on the following specialization:
1. Marketing
2. Finance
3. Human Resource
4. Hospital Administration & Healthcare Management
5. Artificial Intelligence and Data Science
6. Business Analytics
7. Fintech
8. Innovation, Entrepreneurship & Venture Development

### The Executive Programs offered by IMAS for Working Professionals are:
**Post Graduate Diploma in Management (approved by AICTE)**, a 18 to 24 months on-campus and online blended learning program on the following specialization:
1. Marketing
2. Finance
3. Human Resource
4. Hospital Administration & Healthcare Management
5. Artificial Intelligence and Data Science
6. Business Analytics
7. Fintech
8. Logistics & Supply Chain Management
9. Operation Management
10. Agri Business Management

## 4. What is the duration of the PGDM program?
The PGDM program at IMAS typically spans two years, providing students with comprehensive knowledge and skills in management. The Executive PGDM Program is 18-24 months on-campus and online blended learning program.

## 5. What is the admission process for the PGDM program?
Admission is based on the scores achieved in the entrance exam or the IMAS Admission Test (IMASAT) followed by Personal Interview.

## 6. Are there any scholarships or financial aid options available?
Yes, IMAS provides various scholarships and financial aid options to eligible students.

## 7. Does IMAS provide Internship to all students?
IMAS offers Four (4) internships opportunities to all enrolled students as part of their regular PGDM programs:
- Summer Internship
- Winter Internship
- Research Internship
- CSR Internship

Internships usually run across all semesters spanning 4–12 weeks (1-3 months). The Placement & Industry Alliance Cell collaborates with corporate partners for Internships and live projects, offering students industry insight and real-world exposure.

## 8. Does IMAS provide any Education Loan Facility?
IMAS is tied-up with Bank of India to provide a hassle-free education loan facility from the campus itself.
Students can also avail the West Bengal Student Credit Card facility at IMAS.

## 9. Does IMAS provide Placement Opportunity to all students?
This is gonna be an interesting part for you. The Placement assistance cell of IMAS makes rigorous not only in organising internships, corporate trips, grooming sessions but also in organising placement drives so that students land in MNCs at a handsome salary. IMAS placements provide students with industry exposure, hands-on experience, and networking opportunities, enhancing career prospects in analytics, management, and strategy across various sectors.

IMAS provides extensive placement avenues in sectors like BFSI, FMCG, Agri Business, Hospitality, Infrastructure, IT, Healthcare, and many more.

## 10. Does IMAS provide Hostel Facilities?
IMAS provides accommodation facilities (separate for boys and girls) for outstation students who opt for boarding and lodging at a nominal cost, located very near to the campus. Please contact the Office of Dean of Student Welfare for the same.
