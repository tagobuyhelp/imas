# 🌐 MBA Global Program FAQs

## 1. What is the MBA Global program at IMAS?
The MBA Global program provides students with a global perspective on business management. It includes an international pathway where students can study in the UK, gaining global exposure and experience.

## 2. What is the duration of the MBA Global program?
The program is structured to be completed in two years, with the first year executed at IMAS Kolkata Campus, focusing on foundational business management education and the second year executed at partner University Campus in UK, offering specialized global insights.

## 3. What are the eligibility criteria for the MBA Global program?
Candidates should have a graduate degree in any stream with at least 70% marks. Candidates are also required to clear IELTS with band > 6.5. Specific eligibility criteria may vary.

## 4. What is the admission process for the MBA Global program?
Admission is based on IMASAT scores or other equivalent entrance exam scores followed by Personal Interview.

## 5. Are there any scholarships or financial aid options available for the MBA Global program?
Yes, IMAS offers scholarships and financial aid options for eligible students.

## 6. What are the Key Benefits of a MBA Global from IMAS?
- **Save Money**: Save over Rs. 12 lakhs approx. compared to a full 2-year degree in the UK.
- **Dual Qualification**: Get a PGDM from IMAS and a UK Extended Diploma in your first year.
- **UK Work Visa**: Gain post-study work rights in the UK for up to 2 years after graduation.
- **High Earning Potential**: Graduate with an average salary expectation of Rs. 28-34 lakhs Globally.
- **Full Support**: Get help with university applications, visas, and even IELTS preparation.
